# Welcome, Parents and Guardians!

**Make Your First Game in Godot** is a 12-lesson course for children aged 8–10. Each lesson is about 45 minutes long.

## What Is Godot?
Godot is a free program for making video games. Professionals use it, and it is also friendly enough for beginners. In this course your child uses it to build a small game called **Coin Quest**: a hero walks around a room, collects coins, and avoids enemies.

## What Will My Child Learn?
By the end of the course your child can:
- Find their way around the Godot editor and run a game
- Explain that a game is built from **nodes** and **scenes**
- Make a character move, stop at walls and collect things
- Change a few lines of simple code and predict what happens
- Use a **variable** to keep score and lives
- Add sound and simple effects
- Plan, change and share their own version of the game

## How the Lessons Work
- Most of the work is clicking and dragging in the visual editor.
- The code is short, and kids fill in small gaps in code that is already there.
- Every lesson ends with something they can play.
- Every lesson has a "make it yours" step where kids choose colors, pictures, sounds and ideas.
- There is one facilitator for every 6 children.

## How You Can Help
- Ask your child to **show you** their game. Kids learn by explaining.
- Use the question on each sheet to start a conversation.
- Praise effort and ideas, not just the result.
- It is fine if your child makes mistakes. Fixing them is part of learning.

## Playing at Home (Optional)
Godot is free, and it runs on Windows, macOS and Linux. You can download it from the official website (https://godotengine.org/download). Ask the facilitator for the version used in class and for the lesson projects. Home practice is **optional**. Everything needed is provided in class.

## Safe and Age-Appropriate Use
- Children only use the course projects and pictures provided in class.
- We ask children not to share personal information (such as addresses, phone numbers or photos) in their games.
- If you plan to let your child download tools or assets at home, please do it together and use the official websites.
- Any photos or videos of the showcase are taken only if the organization's policy and your permission allow it.
