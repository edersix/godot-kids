# Parent Handout: Lesson 1, Meet Godot

## What We Did Today
We opened Godot for the first time. We learned the main parts of the editor, changed a message on the screen, and added a colored background. Then we ran our first program.

## Words We Learned
- **Node**: a building block with one job (show words, show a picture, play a sound)
- **Scene**: a group of nodes that work together
- **Inspector**: the panel where we change a node's settings
- **Run**: start the game to see what we made

## Ask Your Child
"What is a node? Can you show me your message?"

## Optional Home Activity
Think of a game you love. Draw it, and list three things it is made of (a character, a place, a sound...). Those are like nodes!
