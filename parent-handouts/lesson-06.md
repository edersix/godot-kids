# Parent Handout: Lesson 6, Keep Score

## What We Did Today
We added a score to the screen. Each coin now sends a signal, and the game listens and adds one point.

## Words We Learned
- **Label**: a node that shows words on the screen
- **HUD**: the words and numbers drawn on top of the game
- **Score**: a variable (a box) that remembers the points
- **Emit**: send a signal

## Ask Your Child
"How does the game know a coin was collected? Who sends the message and who listens?"

## Optional Home Activity
Keep a score for a family game, such as a card game or a board game. Talk about how a computer keeps score: it has a box that remembers a number and adds to it.
