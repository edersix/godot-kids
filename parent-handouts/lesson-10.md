# Parent Handout: Lesson 10, Make It Shine

## What We Did Today
We made the coins bob up and down and burst into sparkles when collected. We learned about animation and particles.

## Words We Learned
- **Animation**: changing something over time, like a flip-book
- **AnimationPlayer**: a node that plays animations
- **Keyframe**: a snapshot in an animation ("at this time, it looks like this")
- **Particles**: lots of tiny pictures that burst out, like sparks

## Ask Your Child
"What is a keyframe? How did you make the coin bob?"

## Optional Home Activity
Make a flip-book: draw a small change on each corner of the pages of a notebook (a ball moving, a flower opening) and flip through them quickly.
