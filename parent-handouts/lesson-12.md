# Parent Handout: Lesson 12, Showcase and Next Steps

## What We Did Today
Your child finished their own game, played their friends' games, shared kind feedback, and showed their game to the group. Every child receives a certificate.

## Words We Learned
- **Playtest**: let someone play your game
- **Feedback**: what a player says about your game
- **Showcase**: a time to show your work
- **Developer**: a person who makes games or programs

## Ask Your Child
"What are you most proud of? What would you add if you had more time?"

## What Next?
- Ask your child to **play their game for you** and explain how it works.
- Godot is free: your child can keep experimenting at home (https://godotengine.org/download). Ask the facilitator for the lesson projects.
- Official Godot learning material is available on the Godot website.
- If your child enjoyed the course, encourage them to change something new each week.

## Thank You!
Thank you for supporting your child's learning. We would love your feedback: please tell the facilitator what your child enjoyed and what we could improve.
