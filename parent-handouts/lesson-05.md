# Parent Handout: Lesson 5, Collect Coins

## What We Did Today
We made coins that disappear when the hero touches them. We learned about **signals**: messages that a node sends when something happens.

## Words We Learned
- **Area2D**: a node that notices when something enters it, but does not block it
- **Signal**: a message: "Something happened!"
- **if**: only do something when a question has the answer "yes"
- **queue_free()**: remove something from the game

## Ask Your Child
"What is a signal? If you collect one coin, why do the other coins stay?"

## Optional Home Activity
Find a signal at home: a doorbell, an alarm clock, a phone notification. Who sends it and who listens?
