# Parent Handout: Lesson 11, My Own Game (Part 1)

## What We Did Today
Your child planned their own version of the game on a Game Design Card (name, story, rules, a map of the room) and started changing the game to match their plan.

## Words We Learned
- **Design**: a plan for how a game looks and works
- **Theme**: the story or world of a game
- **Rule**: something the game always does
- **Remix**: take a game and change it to make it yours

## Ask Your Child
"What is your game called? What does your hero collect and what is dangerous?"

## Optional Home Activity
Invent a board game with your child using paper and coins. Write down the rules together, play, and then change one rule. How does the game change?
