# Lesson 11: My Own Game (Part 1)

---

# Today's Goal

> "Today I will plan my own version of the game and start changing it."

---

# Game Makers Plan First

A plan says:

- What is the **story**?
- What are the **rules**?
- What does the **player** do?

---

# New Words

- **Design**: a plan for how your game looks and works
- **Theme**: the story or world (space, ocean, castle...)
- **Rule**: something the game always does
- **Remix**: take a game and change it to make it yours

---

# What If...?

- ...the hero is an astronaut collecting stars?
- ...the enemies are sharks and the room is the sea?
- ...the hero is a cat collecting fish?

---

# Game Design Card

Fill in:

1. My game's name
2. My story: hero, goal, place
3. My rules: what to collect, what is dangerous, how many lives
4. A map of my room
5. My changes

---

# Menu of Changes: Easy

- New background color
- New words in the labels
- New picture for the hero, coin or enemy
- More coins, more enemies

---

# Menu of Changes: Medium

- Faster or slower hero (`speed`)
- A different number of lives
- New walls or a maze
- New sounds or music

---

# Menu of Changes: Challenge

- Coins worth more points
- A faster enemy

---

# Let's Build!

1. Run your game
2. Pick **2 or 3** changes from the menu
3. Do the easy ones first
4. Test after **every** change
5. **Save** often (Ctrl+S)

---

# Checkpoint

Hands up when you have run your changed game at least once.

---

# Stuck?

1. Which part of your plan are you building?
2. Undo with **Ctrl+Z**
3. Ask your neighbor, then the facilitator

---

# Great Work!

- What did you change?
- What do you want to change next time?
- Next lesson: finish and show your game!
