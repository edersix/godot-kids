# Lesson 12: My Own Game (Part 2) and Showcase

---

# Today's Goal

> "Today I will finish my game and show it to my friends."

---

# Making a Game Is a Loop

**Plan** then **Build** then **Test** then **Share**

Then make it better!

---

# New Words

- **Playtest**: let someone play your game
- **Feedback**: what a player says about your game
- **Showcase**: a time to show your work
- **Developer**: a person who makes games

---

# Today's Plan

1. **Build** (20 minutes): finish and polish your game
2. **Playtest** (12 minutes): play a friend's game
3. **Showcase**: tell us about your game
4. **Certificates**!

---

# Build Time

- Open your lesson 11 project
- Read your Game Design Card
- Test from start to Game Over
- **Save** often

---

# Playtest Rules

When you play a friend's game, say:

1. **One thing you liked**
2. **One idea**

We never say "that is bad".

---

# Showcase

Tell us:

- The name of your game
- One sentence about it
